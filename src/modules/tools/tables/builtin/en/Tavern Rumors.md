# Tavern Rumors

- The miller cuts his flour with chalk — two people have already fallen ill.
- Someone sings at night down in the old well.
- The headman's son ran off with a troupe of travelling players.
- A whole wagon train vanished on the north road — no people, no horses.
- The temple priest walks to the graveyard every night.
- Someone is buying up every silver spoon in the county.
- A stag with burning eyes was seen in the forest.
- The guard was replaced a week ago — the new ones don't know anyone by sight.
- The witch from the marsh is taking commissions again.
- The innkeeper keeps something in the cellar that scratches at the door.
- Not everyone came back from the mines — and those who did won't talk.
- A merchant from the capital is hiring guards and paying double.
- A fish caught in the river had a gold ring in its belly.
- They say the countess died long ago and a double rules in her place.
- The goblins from the hills want to trade instead of raid.
- The old lighthouse lit itself.
- A peddler at the fair is selling dreams.
- The smith forged a sword and hasn't slept since.
- The children sing a new counting rhyme — and no one knows who made it up.
- A hundred gold for the one-eyed thief, dead or alive.
