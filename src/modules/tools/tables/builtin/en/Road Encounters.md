12: Pilgrims on their way to a holy site invite you along.
13: The bridge is washed out: find a ford or lose half a day.
14: Ambush: {1d4+1} bandits demand a "road fee".

16: A wolf pack keeps its distance and follows.

18: A wanderer offers to tell your fortune — and guesses far too much.
19: An abandoned shrine; fresh flowers on the altar.
