# Weather

1-3: Clear, a light breeze.
4-5: Overcast and cool.
6-7: Fog until noon.
8-9: Drizzle all day.
10: Thunderstorm: travel takes twice as long.
11: Strong wind: ranged attacks have disadvantage, no fire without shelter.
12: Something odd — {hail the size of walnuts|a blood-red sunset|snow in the middle of summer|silence: no wind, no birds}.
