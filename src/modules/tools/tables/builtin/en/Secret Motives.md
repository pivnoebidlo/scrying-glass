# Secret Motives

- Owes a large sum to dangerous people
- Spies for the local lord
- Searching for a missing brother
- Hiding a stolen relic
- Wants revenge on one of the heroes (or on someone a hero resembles)
- Belongs to a forbidden cult — and already regrets it
- In love and ready to do something foolish
- Hides that they are a werewolf
- Wants to get out of town at any cost
- Is preparing a poisoning
- Knows where a treasure is hidden but is afraid to go there
- Forges documents
- Secretly helps the highway robbers
- Wants their boss's job
- Looking for a cure for a sick child
- Received a strange letter and doesn't know what to do with it
- Saw what they shouldn't have and fears the witnesses
- Dreams of becoming an adventurer
- Pretends to be someone else — the real owner of the name is dead
- Sells rumors to the highest bidder
