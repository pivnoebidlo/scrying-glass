# Forged in the Dark — package

The system pack (`system/fitd.json`) and its compendium, built by `scripts/build-fitd.ts` (`npm run build-fitd`) from
`scripts/fitd.<lang>.md`: the texts are edited there. `npm run build-content` packs it; the official author publishes
it. Raise `release` in `content.json` when the files change.

This work is based on Blades in the Dark (found at http://www.bladesinthedark.com/), product of One Seven Design,
developed and authored by John Harper, and licensed for our use under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported
license (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

The English text (`fitd.en.json`) is a retelling of the Blades in the Dark SRD (https://bladesinthedark.com/basics)
written for Scrying Glass; the setting of Blades in the Dark (Duskwall and the rest) is not used. This is not an
official product and is not endorsed by One Seven Design.

Русский текст (`fitd.ru.json`) — пересказ и перевод SRD Blades in the Dark, сделанный для Scrying Glass, без мира
оригинальной игры. Термины — свои (открытого русского перевода нет).
